    About A*

    f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

    g(n) – the actual cost travelled from the person to cell n. Every cell entered adds its cost (safe 1, crowded 3, smoke 5, high risk 10).

    h(n) – the estimated cost from n to the exit, using Manhattan distance |x1 − x2| + |y1 − y2|. It never over-estimates, because every cell costs at least 1.

    f(n) – the combined estimate. A* always expands the cell with the lowest f(n) first.

    Why A* suits evacuation

    Dijkstra explores in every direction. A* is guided towards the exits by h(n), so it finds the cheapest route after looking at far fewer cells. Because the heuristic is admissible, the route it returns is the lowest-cost route, which makes it fast enough to re-run every second as the fire spreads.

    Why not just the shortest path?

    Route 1: 6 cells, 3 of them smoke → cost 3×1 + 3×5 = 18.
    Route 2: 8 cells, all safe → cost 8×1 = 8.
    A* picks Route 2. It is longer but much safer.
